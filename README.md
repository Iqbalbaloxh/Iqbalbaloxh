# Hello, I'm Iqbal 👋

### Independent Developer | AI Safety & LLM Evaluation Enthusiast

I'm an independent developer exploring how to make large language models more reliable, transparent, and trustworthy.

My current interests include AI safety, alignment evaluation, factual accuracy, hallucination detection, multilingual reasoning, and prompt-injection resistance.

## 🔬 Featured Research Project

### [Grounded LLM Safety & Alignment Lab](https://github.com/Iqbalbaloxh/grounded-llm-safety-alignment-lab)

An open-source research prototype for evaluating how language models interpret external evidence and respond to potentially misleading or untrusted information.

**Research focus:**
- Evidence-grounded responses and hallucination analysis
- Calibrated uncertainty and appropriate abstention
- Prompt-injection resistance in untrusted source material
- Multilingual evaluation across English, Urdu, and Roman Urdu
- Reproducible AI evaluation methods

## 🧪 Current Project Status

The repository contains an initial evaluation framework, synthetic test cases, offline scoring tools, and a proposed research methodology.

This is an early-stage research prototype. Real-world Claude evaluation findings have not yet been established.

## 🎯 Research Goals

My goal is to investigate practical methods for identifying unsupported model claims, evaluating trustworthy reasoning, and improving the reliability of AI-assisted information analysis.

I'm interested in responsible AI development, open research, and reproducible experimentation.
